# Shapes

A **shape** is the fixed skeleton of one kind of document. Every document of a kind has the same
parts in the same order, so a reader who has seen one knows where to look in the next. That is
the skeletal-notes effect at the scale of a collection
([learning science](../evidence/learning-science/)). Each shape names the principles it applies.

## Contents

| Shape | For |
|---|---|
| [note.md](note.md) | one topic, learned or worked on: a course note, a research note, a concept |
| [readme-and-folder.md](readme-and-folder.md) | what a folder holds and how to use it, read by someone who arrived from a search |
| [report.md](report.md) | a solved problem explained step by step, so the reader can solve the next one |
| [commit.md](commit.md) | why a change was made, in the version history |

## Choosing a shape

- One document, one shape. A text that needs two shapes is two documents ([one mode per document](../principles/document/one-mode-per-document.md)).
- When no shape fits, write to the [standard](../standard.md) and propose a shape once three documents of the new kind exist.
